800 px wide and stored in `assets/images/recipes/`. Each photo's credit also
shows at the bottom of its Recipe Detail screen. Some photos show a closely
related dish (for example, fried egg tofu for Tokwa't Itlog) because no
freely licensed photo of the exact recipe was available.
